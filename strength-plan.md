# Strength Plan Reference

> Running plans reference strength sessions (💪) but the actual routine lived only in Todoist.
> This is the in-repo reference so agents know what "Kraft Tag X" means.

## System

- **Method**: Mark Lauren (bodyweight), evening home sessions, 20–30 min.
- **Frequency**: 2–3× / week, aligned with quality-run days (typically Mon/Wed evenings).
- **Never**: heavy foot/jump load right after a toe/foot injury (see `injuries.md`).

## Session Split

| Tag | Focus | Emoji |
|-----|-------|-------|
| Tag 1 | Drücken (push) | 💪 |
| Tag 2 | Beine & Gesäß (legs/glutes) | 💪 |
| Tag 3 | Ziehen (pull) | 💪 |

Alt naming seen in logs: "Full Body Focus", "Lower Body + Hinge Power", "Upper Body Skills".

## Scheduling Rules

- Same day as runs OK, but **never Monday morning before intervals**; put strength in the evening.
- Post-injury: skip jumps/plyo and heavy push-off, keep upper-body + core.
- Trail-specific: prioritize glutes/hip flexors/calves for climbing power and quad durability.
