# Fueling

> Carb intake strategy per session type. Referenced by plans and the build plan's fuel rule.

**Last updated**: 2026-08-20

## Product

- **No Carbs, No Glory — Race Blend** (carb powder, mix in bottle/flask).
- **Carb ratio**: 1:0.8 (Maltodextrin/Dextrose : Fructose) — optimized gut absorption at high dosing.
- Designed for up to **90 g/h**; with a trained gut up to **120 g/h** possible.
- Contains **sodium** (sea salt) for electrolyte/cramp prevention + **BCAAs** (leucine/isoleucine/valine) to delay muscle fatigue.
- Mild flavor — avoids taste fatigue under high intake / heat. Flavors: e.g. mango, lemon.

## Dosing (powder to hit carb target)

| Carb target | Powder / h | Note |
|-------------|-----------|------|
| ~60 g/h | ~63 g/h | ~1 portion — recovery + long runs |
| ~90 g/h | ~95 g/h | ~1.6 portions — hard sessions |
| ~120 g/h | ~126 g/h | trained-gut ceiling, race-day option |

## Targets (carbs per hour)

| Session | Target | Note |
|---------|--------|------|
| Recovery runs | ~60 g/h | keep gut used to intake even on easy days |
| Long runs | ~60 g/h | steady, train the gut for race duration |
| Harder sessions (quality/intervals/tempo) | ~90 g/h | higher demand, practice race-day upper range |

## Rules

- **Train the gut**: take carbs even on easy/recovery runs so the stomach tolerates race-day volume.
- **Long runs (from W39)**: rehearse full race fueling at ~60 g/h with the actual product.
- **Race day**: product supports up to 90 g/h (120 g/h trained gut) — an option if the gut tolerates it in peak-week long runs.
- Mix and dose the powder to hit the g/h target for the session's duration.
